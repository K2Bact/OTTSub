OTTSub · for desktop Chrome

Two subtitle tracks. Your timing.

OTTSub is a free desktop Chrome extension that adjusts Netflix subtitle timing in 0.1-second steps and displays two subtitle tracks available for the title you are watching.

View OTTSub in the Chrome Web Store ↗

OTTSub is free. A Netflix account and subscription are required separately.

One panel, two independent tracks

Fine-tune each language

Adjust primary and secondary subtitle timing separately, in 0.1-second steps. Reset either track to 0.0s when needed.

Use the subtitles Netflix provides

Choose two available tracks for the current title. OTTSub does not generate translations or unlock missing languages.

Make room for learning

Choose subtitle size, color and background. Blur one or both tracks, reveal them on hover, or enable hover-to-pause.

Start with a video on Netflix

  1. Install on your computer

    Open the OTTSub Chrome Web Store page in desktop Chrome and install the extension.

  2. Open the side panel

    Play a video on the Netflix website. Click the OTTSub extension icon to open its side panel.

  3. Choose and adjust

    Select the primary and secondary subtitle tracks available for that title. Adjust their timing separately with the panel controls or shortcuts.

Subtitles 0.3 seconds late?

Starting from 0.0s, try +0.3s for the affected subtitle track to display it earlier. If it appears 0.3 seconds too early, try −0.3s instead. Listen, compare and fine-tune; a single offset may not fix a track that drifts over time.

Subtitles appear late+0.3sDisplay them earlier
Subtitles appear early−0.3sDisplay them later

Example values start at 0.0s. Primary and secondary offsets are independent. Adjusting subtitle timing does not change the video's playback speed.

Before you install

How can I fix Netflix subtitles that are out of sync?

In OTTSub, adjust the affected subtitle track in 0.1-second steps. Starting from 0.0s, try +0.3s when subtitles appear 0.3 seconds late, or −0.3s when they appear 0.3 seconds early. Primary and secondary timing can be adjusted independently.

Why do subtitles drift out of sync again after I adjust them?

A fixed timing offset can correct a constant early or late subtitle track. If the gap keeps growing during playback, one fixed offset may not solve it. Check another subtitle track provided by Netflix; OTTSub does not rewrite subtitle timings or change video playback speed.

Can OTTSub show two languages on Netflix?

Yes, when both subtitle tracks are available for the current Netflix title and account. Choose the primary and secondary tracks in the side panel; their timing and appearance can be adjusted separately.

Does OTTSub translate subtitles automatically?

No. OTTSub displays subtitle tracks provided by Netflix. It does not create AI translations, provide a translation service, or add languages that Netflix does not offer for that title.

Why is my subtitle language missing?

Subtitle availability depends on the Netflix title and account. Check the languages Netflix provides for that title. Changing the OTTSub menu language does not add or translate subtitle tracks.

Are the extension and Netflix both free?

OTTSub itself is free and does not require a separate OTTSub account or subscription. A Netflix account and subscription are required separately and are not included.

Does OTTSub work in mobile Chrome, Safari or the Netflix app?

This package targets desktop Chrome on the Netflix website. It does not support mobile Chrome, Safari or operation inside the Netflix app. Being able to view this website on a phone does not mean the extension runs there.

What about Microsoft Edge and Mac?

Microsoft Edge on Windows has passed local compatibility tests. macOS has Option shortcuts and has been partially verified; complete Mac verification is pending. Other Chromium browsers have not been verified.

Can I choose ordinary subtitles or CC/SDH?

When the title provides them, OTTSub keeps ordinary subtitle and CC/SDH tracks separate. CC/SDH can include sound descriptions and speaker information. Track availability still depends on Netflix.

Does OTTSub send subtitles to an AI service?

No AI translation service is used. Subtitle tracks are retrieved from Netflix services and processed in your browser; preferences use Chrome storage. The store build excludes the local diagnostic recorder, analytics and viewing-history tracking tools. This does not mean the extension makes no network requests.

Is OTTSub an official Netflix product?

No. OTTSub is an independently developed extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix.

Privacy policy

This policy describes the OTTSub Chrome Web Store build and this product website. OTTSub is provided by K2Bact. Subtitle processing happens in your browser; this does not mean that the extension handles no data or makes no network requests.

Data used for subtitle display

OTTSub reads subtitle-track metadata and addresses, language and CC/SDH labels, subtitle text and timing, the current Netflix title identifier, and playback state needed for timing and display. These are used to provide subtitle synchronization and dual subtitles. OTTSub does not create a viewing-history database or save subtitle text in its settings storage.

Preferences and Chrome storage

Language, timing, appearance and learning preferences, together with technical values used to order settings changes, are saved using Chrome storage. The existing implementation uses local and sync storage. Depending on your Chrome settings, Google Chrome may synchronize those preference values through its own service. Subtitle text and Netflix subtitle addresses are not included in those preference records.

Network requests and recipients

The extension retrieves subtitle tracks from Netflix services over HTTPS, and the browser and Netflix apply their own network and account policies. The store build contains no developer-operated collection endpoint, analytics tracker, AI translation service, or subtitle upload feature. OTTSub does not read account passwords or use the Chrome cookies API. Optional support links open Ctee or Buy Me a Coffee only when you choose them; those sites apply their own policies.

Purpose and Limited Use

OTTSub uses the data it accesses only for its disclosed subtitle-timing and dual-subtitle features. This use follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The extension does not sell this data, use it for advertising or profiling, or send it to an AI training or translation service.

Retention and your controls

Subtitle and playback working data are held in page memory and released when the page is reloaded or its tab is closed. Browser-managed network caches are separate from OTTSub settings. Preferences persist in Chrome storage; use Chrome's extension and storage controls to manage or remove them. Clearing ordinary browsing history alone may not clear extension settings. Copies managed by Chrome Sync are governed by your Chrome settings and Google's policies; the developer cannot directly inspect or delete them.

This website

This static website has no analytics scripts, account forms or external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes even when they are not signed in. Following links to Netflix, the Chrome Web Store or other services is subject to those services' policies. Links to the Chrome Web Store include fixed campaign labels to distinguish this website, its language and link position in store reports. These labels contain no personal identifier, subtitle text or viewing history.

Questions and changes

Contacting the developer voluntarily may disclose your email address and the message you send; do not include passwords, cookies, access tokens or raw viewing diagnostics. Material changes to the extension's data practices require updated disclosures; this policy describes the reviewed implementation, rather than making a claim about unrelated developer tools.
